Building Permit Applications

Did you know?

Building without a permit can mean fines, stop-work orders, and real legal headaches. If there's ever a fire, flood, or injury, an unpermitted project can void your insurance coverage entirely — getting it right from the start is always the cheaper path.

Municipalities have full authority to enforce the Ontario Building Code and local zoning bylaws, including stop-work orders and fines that can run into the thousands. Starting permit-ready protects your budget and your timeline.

Permits are typically required for:

Pools, decks, sheds and prefab structures, detached garages and carports, pergolas and gazebos, demolition, basement finishing, additions and renovations, foundation repair and waterproofing, change of use and conversions, and correcting fire code violations.
